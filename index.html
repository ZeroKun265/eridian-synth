Cheat sheet

After a word (stackable)

  • ! louder
  • ? glide up
  • _ glide down
  • * longer
  • ~ wobble

e.g. no***!!   what?   oh~~

Pauses: , . ; :

Tone tags (affect following words)

  • [n] neutral
  • [h] happy
  • [s] sad
  • [vs] or [very sad]
  • [sc] scared
  • [e] excited
  • [a] angry

The buttons above set the starting tone.
